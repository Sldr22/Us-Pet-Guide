Bringing home a new pet: a simple starter checklist

A little preparation can help your new dog or cat settle in more comfortably. Needs differ by species, age, size, and health, so ask your veterinarian or adoption organization for pet-specific advice.

Prepare a quiet space

  • Choose a calm area where your pet can rest without being disturbed.
  • Keep electrical cords, cleaning products, medicines, and small swallowable objects out of reach.
  • For cats, prepare a litter area away from food and water.

Gather the basics

  • Food appropriate for your pet's age and needs.
  • Clean food and water bowls.
  • A comfortable bed or resting place.
  • Species-appropriate toys and enrichment.
  • For dogs: a suitable leash, collar or harness, and identification tag.
  • For cats: a secure carrier, litter box, and suitable litter.

Plan veterinary care

Arrange an initial veterinary visit and ask about vaccinations, parasite prevention, microchipping, and any medical records your pet may need. Do not give human medication unless a veterinarian specifically instructs you to.

Give your pet time

Keep the first days calm. Introduce new people, rooms, and other animals gradually. Use patient, positive interactions and maintain a predictable routine.

This article is general educational information, not veterinary advice. Contact a veterinarian for individual guidance.
